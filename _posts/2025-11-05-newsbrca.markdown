British Radio Car Association (BRCA) has made an [important membership update announcement](https://www.brca.org/racer-hub/brca-articles/important-membership-update-action-required-before-1st-january-2026) for all members. All members must complete a password reset and renew their membership for the 2026 season before 1st January 2026.

Thank you for your cooperation and continued support in keeping the BRCA’s systems safe, secure, and future-ready.
