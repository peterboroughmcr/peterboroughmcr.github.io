Peterborough Model Car Racing Club (PMCR) drivers attended [King of Clubs RC Series 2026](https://www.facebook.com/kocrcseries). King of Clubs brings together Adur Model Car Club, Aldershot Model Car Club, Eastbourne Electric Car Club and West London Racing Centre, working in collaboration with RC Vision and the BRCA to deliver competitive, structured and professionally run race days across the region for everyone from first-time Rookies to experienced championship contenders.
