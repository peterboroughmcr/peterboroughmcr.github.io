Dates for the BRCA 2026 10EC 2 Wheel Drive National Championship have been announced. The championship will be contested over 5 outdoor rounds on tarmac, visiting many of the best outdoor tracks in the UK.

Cotswolds, 26th April 2026  
Stafford, 24th May 2026  
Halifax, 28th June 2026  
West London, 2nd August 2026  
Adur, 6th September 2026
